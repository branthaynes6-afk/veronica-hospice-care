COMFORT & SUPPORT THERAPIES

Supportive options designed around comfort, connection, and personal preferences.

Care 4 You Senior Services Home Health and Hospice offers supportive therapies that may complement a patient’s hospice plan of care. Select an option below to learn more about the type of comfort and support it can provide.

Music Therapy

Music-based support may use familiar songs, listening, singing, or other appropriate musical experiences to encourage relaxation, connection, expression, and meaningful moments for patients and families.

Pet Therapy

Animal-assisted visits may offer companionship, gentle interaction, and comforting moments for patients who enjoy animals, when appropriate for the patient, care setting, and plan of care.

Aromatherapy

When appropriate, approved gentle scents may be used to help create a calm and comforting environment. Aromatherapy is considered only when suitable for the patient’s preferences, sensitivities, and care plan.

Gentle Massage

Light, comfort-focused massage or gentle touch may be offered to promote relaxation and comfort when permitted by the patient’s condition, care plan, preferences, and care setting.

Chaplain & Spiritual Support

Chaplain and spiritual support can provide a respectful space for prayer, reflection, conversation, emotional support, or faith-based guidance in keeping with the patient’s beliefs and wishes, when requested or permitted.

Supportive services may vary based on patient preference, plan of care, safety considerations, facility rules, and availability. These services complement hospice care and do not replace medical care or clinical guidance.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Hospice care questions families often ask.

What is hospice care?

Hospice focuses on comfort, quality of life, and support for people with serious illness and their families.

When should a family consider hospice?

Families often begin asking about hospice when a serious illness is progressing and the focus of care may be shifting toward comfort and quality of life. A qualified hospice team determines hospice eligibility.

Does contacting Veronica mean we are committing to hospice?

No. Reaching out is simply a way to ask hospice questions and understand available options. A qualified hospice team determines eligibility and develops any plan of care.

Can I call about a family member?

Yes. You can reach out with general hospice questions. Please avoid sending sensitive medical information through regular email or text unless you have been directed to a secure method.

What supportive therapies may be available?

Depending on the patient’s preferences, plan of care, safety considerations, facility rules, and availability, supportive options may include music therapy, pet therapy, aromatherapy, gentle massage, and chaplain or spiritual support.

What areas does Veronica serve?

Veronica works with families throughout Houston, Katy, Texas Medical Center, The Heights, Downtown Houston, Galleria, Memorial, West Houston, and surrounding areas.
