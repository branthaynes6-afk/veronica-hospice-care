COMMON QUESTIONS

Hospice care questions families often ask.

What is hospice care?

Hospice focuses on comfort, quality of life, and support for people with serious illness and their families.

When should a family consider hospice?

Families often begin asking about hospice when a serious illness is progressing and the focus of care may be shifting toward comfort and quality of life. A qualified hospice team determines eligibility.

Does contacting Veronica mean we are committing to hospice?

No. Reaching out is simply a way to ask questions and understand available options. A qualified hospice team determines eligibility and develops any plan of care.

Can I call about a family member?

Yes. You can reach out with general questions. Please avoid sending sensitive medical information through regular email or text unless you have been directed to a secure method.

What areas does Veronica serve?

Veronica works with families throughout Houston, Katy, Texas Medical Center, The Heights, Downtown Houston, Galleria, Memorial, West Houston, and surrounding areas.
